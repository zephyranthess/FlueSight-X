Support

FlueSight X

Questions, feedback or a problem? Write to support@norfa.fun and include your device model and iOS version if something is not working.

Common questions

  • What is FlueSight X?
    A chimney inspection and reporting app for chimney sweeps and inspectors. You record an inspection on site (checklist, findings, photos), produce a PDF report for the customer, prepare a quote and schedule the work.
  • Do I need an account?
    No. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no server. Everything you enter stays on your device.
  • Is my data collected or sent anywhere?
    No. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking and does not connect to the internet. Read the full Privacy Policy.
  • Where are my inspections and photos stored?
    In private storage on your iPhone or iPad. Because nothing is uploaded, deleting the app deletes its data, and the app cannot recover it for you. Export customer and inspection lists as CSV from Settings, Data, and keep the PDF reports you have shared.
  • How do I send a report to a customer?
    Open the inspection, tap Report, then share the PDF with Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files. The app only shares what you choose to send.
  • Can I change the inspection level during an inspection?
    Yes. Tap the Inspection level row at the top of the checklist. Checks that apply only to a higher level are added or removed, and anything you have already recorded is kept.
  • Does the app tell me whether a chimney is safe?
    No. The checklist, findings library and suggested wording are tools to help a qualified professional work faster. You decide and sign off every finding. Review the content against the rules that apply where you work.
  • How do I turn on reminders?
    Open Settings, Reminders. The app asks for notification permission the first time. Reminders are scheduled on the device and need no internet connection.
  • Which devices are supported?
    iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
