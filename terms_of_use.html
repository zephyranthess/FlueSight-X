2What the App is, and what it is not

FlueSight X helps chimney sweeps and inspectors to:

  • walk a chimney with a guided checklist and record the condition of each part
  • take, import and mark up photos
  • choose written findings from a library they control
  • make a repair quote that a customer can approve and sign on the screen
  • make PDF reports, and keep customers, properties, jobs and reminders

The App is a tool for recording and presenting what you find. It does not look at a chimney, test an appliance or detect defects.
