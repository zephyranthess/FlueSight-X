Privacy Policy

FlueSight X · Last updated October 9, 2026

This policy explains what FlueSight X does with information. It is short because the app was built so that your information never reaches us.

In short

  • No account and no servers. The app works entirely on your device. There is nothing to sign in to.
  • We collect nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting that leaves your device. We never see your customers, photos, reports or quotes.
  • Your data leaves only when you send it. A report, a quote or a spreadsheet goes out when you choose to share it, to the people you choose.
  • Permissions are used only for what you ask. Camera for photos, photo library for photos you pick, notifications for your own service reminders. You can refuse any of them.
  • You are in control. You can edit and delete anything. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device. Because we keep no copy, we cannot recover it for you.

1Who we are

FlueSight X ("we", "us", "our") is a chimney inspection and reporting app for chimney sweeps and inspectors. It is published by the developer of FlueSight X.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@norfa.fun.

2What the app stores on your device

FlueSight X keeps everything you enter in private storage on your own device. This information is created and controlled by you:

  • Your business details: business name, address, phone, email, licence and certification numbers, logo, inspector names, and the settings for your reports and quotes.
  • Your customers: names, phone numbers, email addresses, property addresses and access notes you type in, and any customer list you import from a spreadsheet.
  • Inspections: the appliances and flues, the status of each part, the findings you choose, your notes, weather, and roof access.
  • Photos: pictures you take or import, the marked-up copies, and original files until a report has been sent (you can keep them longer in Settings).
  • Quotes and signatures: prices, options, totals, and the signature a customer draws on the screen together with the name they type.
  • Reports: the PDF files the app makes for you, including every version.
  • Your schedule and reminders: jobs, dates, and the service reminders you turn on.

None of this is sent to us. If you are a chimney professional, the information about your customers is yours to look after, and you decide what to keep and what to delete.

3What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive, store or process your information on any server. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software that collects data. It does not read your contacts and it does not use your location.

The app keeps a small log on your device that records technical errors only, never customer details. The app never sends it anywhere.

4Permissions the app asks for

The app asks only when you use the feature that needs it, and works without the permission unless noted.

  • Camera. To photograph chimney parts during an inspection. Photos are saved inside the app.
  • Photo library. To import photos you select, such as stills from a chimney camera. The app sees only the photos you pick, not your whole library.
  • Files. To import photos, your logo or a customer list, and to save or share files you export.
  • Notifications. To remind you when a customer is due for service. Reminders are created and delivered by your device. There is no push server and nothing is sent to us.

Voice dictation, if you use it, is handled by your phone's keyboard and is governed by Apple or Google, not by this app. The app never uses your microphone itself.

5When information leaves your device

Only when you do something that sends it:

  • You share or email a report or quote. It goes to the people you choose, through the share sheet or your email app. After that, the recipient's own privacy practices apply.
  • You export a customer or inspection list as a spreadsheet and send or save the file.
  • You tap Navigate. The property address is passed to the maps app you pick (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze). Their privacy policies apply.
  • You tap Call, Text or Email on a customer. The number or address is handed to your phone, messages or mail app.

6Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. It relies on features built into your phone that Apple or Google provide, such as the share sheet, Mail, Maps, the camera, the file picker and notifications. Those features are governed by Apple's or Google's own terms and privacy policies.

If you have turned on iCloud Backup or Google backup for your phone, your phone may include this app's data in that backup, under Apple's or Google's terms. We do not control or see it.

7How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a customer, a property, an inspection, a photo, a quote or a report inside the app, and the files that belong to it are removed with it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Because we keep no copy, we cannot restore anything that has been deleted, lost or damaged. Reports and quotes you have already sent stay with the people you sent them to. Keep your own copies of anything important.

8Children

FlueSight X is a professional tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give people rights to see, correct, delete and move their personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to give you or delete. Everything the app stores is in your hands and can be edited, deleted or exported inside the app.

If you use the app for your work, your customers may have such rights against you. The app helps you meet them: you can find, edit, delete and export a customer's information at any time. Following the privacy law that applies to your business is your responsibility.

10Security

Your information stays in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. Use a passcode, and keep your phone's software up to date. Because the app never sends your data to a server, there is no server for anyone to break into. A report or quote is an ordinary file once you share it, so send it only to the people it is meant for.

11International transfers

We do not transfer your information, because we do not receive it.

12Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be in the app and on our website with a new date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your information, we will say so when you update the app.

13Contact us

Questions about this policy or your privacy: support@norfa.fun.
